RINGFENCE — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
Abuse-Ring Sentinel for Razorpay Buildathon 2026 · Track 02, AI Risk Manager

A two-speed system. The identity graph is slow and batch: it fuses device, IP
and email evidence across every merchant into a card-to-card graph. The scorer
is fast and online: 31 features and a GBM verdict in under 40ms, with a
fully-cited case file attached to every alert.

Full design document: ../ARCHITECTURE.md

LEGEND
------
  Identity Graph    cards are nodes; edges are shared device / IP / email ONLY.
                    No merchant edges: 100.0% of card pairs share a merchant,
                    vs 0.152% sharing an identity value.

  Ring Detector     recall-first ensemble. Louvain at 6 resolutions, connected
                    components at 4 edge-weight floors, and identity-ego sets.
                    Measured ring recall 0.942.

  Feature Engine    31 features (10 structural, 8 identity, 13 behavioural)
                    over the candidate's shared-infrastructure transactions.
                    p99 32.9ms.

  GBM Scorer        LightGBM, leave-one-world-out CV, threshold chosen by
                    minimising rupee cost (C_fp = 2.3L, C_fn = 8.5L) rather
                    than F1. Precision 0.869 / recall 0.964.

  Dossier Builder   deterministic cited evidence first, LLM narrative second.
                    An uncited claim cannot be constructed.

  Case Memory       stores analyst verdicts BOTH ways. Confirmed ring -> +15%
                    precedent boost. Confirmed false positive -> -20% damp.

  Vulcan Layer      noisy-OR of two independent signals. Ringfence's score does
                    not use Vulcan as a feature, so composing them is not
                    counting one signal twice.

BOUNDED AND GATED
-----------------
  Ringfence emits a RECOMMENDATION. It cannot block a payment, decline a card,
  or move money. Automated action requires a Ring Template with more than 10
  analyst-confirmed precedents; every novel-pattern BLOCK returns
  auto_action_allowed=false and human_review_required=true.
